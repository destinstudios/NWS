Privacy policy

This page explains how appointment enquiries through this website are handled. The business owner should review and approve this policy before launch.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Information you provide

If you use the appointment enquiry form, you choose to provide details such as your name, phone number, preferred service, date, time and any additional message. The form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp; it does not submit your information to a database operated by this website.

2. WhatsApp and phone communication

When you continue to WhatsApp or call the spa, your communication is handled by the relevant third-party service and the spa. WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy apply to your use of that service. Please avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information in an enquiry.

3. Website hosting and third-party resources

This website may load fonts, photographs, maps and messaging links from third-party providers. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address or browser data when their resources load. Their privacy policies govern that processing.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website template does not intentionally include an analytics dashboard or advertising tracker. Hosting providers or third-party embedded services may process technical data as part of delivering their service. The owner should update this section if analytics, advertising, booking platforms or other integrations are added.

5. How enquiry information is used

Information you send directly to the spa may be used to respond to your questions and discuss appointment availability. The spa should retain such information only as needed for legitimate business purposes and protect it appropriately.

6. Your choices

You can choose not to use the enquiry form and instead call the spa. You may ask the spa about the personal information it has received from you by contacting it directly.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or how your enquiry information is handled, contact NANDANI WELLNESS SPA at 9156361327 or visit the contact page for the business address.

Owner action required: review this policy for the actual business practices, add a dedicated privacy contact if appropriate, and obtain legal advice where necessary. This template is not legal advice.
